01 / About

What is Gazyava-Coin?

Gazyava-Coin is a community-focused meme token project planned for the Solana blockchain. Our idea is simple: bring people together through crypto culture, humor and creative community activities. We aim to build in public, explain decisions clearly and avoid unrealistic promises.
